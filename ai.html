Company Overview

MushClub is a premium mushroom cultivation and supply company specializing in fresh, organically grown mushrooms for households, restaurants, wholesalers, and commercial growers.

About MushClub

We are dedicated to mycological excellence. Operating out of our climate-controlled facilities in Abishekapatti, Tirunelveli, Tamil Nadu, India, we manage the entire supply chain from spore to door.

Mission

To make fresh, organic mushrooms accessible to everyone — from local kitchens to family dinner tables.

Vision

To be the leading standard in organic mycological cultivation and sustainable farming across the region.

Products

  • Fresh Button Mushrooms: Premium white button mushrooms.
  • Gourmet Oyster Mushrooms: High-quality, organically grown oyster mushrooms.
  • Premium Grain Spawn: Lab-tested grain spawn for commercial growers.
  • 100% Natural Hardwood Substrate Pellets: Chemical-free growth mediums.

Services

  • Organic Mushroom Cultivation: We farm with zero chemicals.
  • Wholesale Supply: Reliable bulk mushroom distribution.
  • Restaurant Supply: We partner with over 250+ restaurants and caterers.
  • Commercial Grower Supply: We provide farmers with spawn and substrates.
  • Spawn Production: In-house laboratory spawn cultivation.

Mushroom Cultivation Process & Quality Standards

100% of our mushrooms are grown in clean, chemical-free organic substrates inside sterile, climate-monitored grow rooms. Every harvest is lab-tested for purity. We maintain strict quality standards to ensure premium quality in every batch.

Delivery Process, Wholesale, & Restaurant Partnerships

We operate a fast cold-chain delivery system. Freshly harvested mushrooms reach our B2B partners (including over 250+ restaurants) and wholesale distributors within 24 hours of harvest.
