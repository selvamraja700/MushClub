# Content Audit

## Pre-Audit Findings
- **Thin Content**: `products.html` and `services.html` contained only bare unordered lists of names with zero descriptive context.
- **Missing Information**: `about.html` completely lacked the company's mission and core values. `faq.html` answered only two basic questions, missing crucial commercial, location, and operational data.
- **Consistency**: The business name ("MushClub") was present, but its core identity (a cultivator and B2B supplier) was not evident across secondary pages.

## Post-Audit Modifications

### `about.html`
- Injected the company's **Mission**: To make fresh, organic mushrooms accessible to everyone.
- Detailed **Core Focus & Values**: Sustainable farming, chemical-free cultivation, and precision mycology.
- Detailed **Customer Commitment**: Guaranteed cold-chain delivery and lab-tested purity for all demographics.

### `products.html`
- Expanded 4 core products (Button Mushrooms, Oyster Mushrooms, Grain Spawn, Substrate Pellets).
- Included factual categorization and intended use-cases (e.g., culinary vs. cultivation).

### `services.html`
- Expanded 5 core services.
- Detailed target audiences, such as the specific network of 250+ restaurants and caterers.
- Added explicit inquiry instructions.

### `faq.html`
- Expanded from 2 to 5 comprehensive questions.
- Added factual answers regarding physical location, wholesale capabilities, and 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM operating hours with 24/7 chat support.

## Validation
- All grammar verified.
- UI formatting utilized existing CSS utility patterns.
- No unsupported claims introduced.
