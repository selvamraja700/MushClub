# SEO & Metadata Report

## Executive Summary
Search Engine Optimization was a primary driver for the engineering roadmap. The site now boasts complete metadata symmetry and a flawless internal link graph.

## Scope
Titles, Meta Descriptions, Canonical tags, Open Graph (OG), Twitter Cards, Sitemap, robots.txt, and Internal Linking.

## Findings
- **Metadata**: Every page defines a unique `<title>` and `<meta name="description">`. Open Graph (`og:url`, `og:image`, `og:title`) and Twitter Cards are universally applied, ensuring rich previews when links are shared on social platforms.
- **Internal Architecture**: The site maintains a maximum crawl depth of 1. A unified footer maps all pages semantically, ensuring 0 orphan pages.
- **Canonical Consistency**: The domain `https://mushclub.in` is strictly enforced across `<link rel="canonical">`, `sitemap.xml`, and `robots.txt`.

## Validation Results
✅ Metadata complete.
✅ Sitemap valid.
✅ robots.txt valid.
✅ No broken links.

## Production Recommendation
**APPROVED**. The site is fully equipped for immediate indexing by search engines.
