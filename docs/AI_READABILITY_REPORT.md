# AI Readability & Extraction Report

## Executive Summary
The platform is optimized not just for human users, but for autonomous LLMs and Search Generative Experiences (SGE).

## Scope
Entity extraction, Business understanding, JSON-LD schemas.

## Findings
- **Structured Data**: `index.html` holds robust `Organization` and `LocalBusiness` schemas mapping the business to its Tirunelveli location.
- **Semantic Density**: Subpages (`products.html`, `services.html`) feature dense, factual paragraphs outlining product categories and B2B services, heavily outperforming legacy bullet-point designs.
- **Explicit Access**: `ai.html`, `ai-context.txt`, and `llms.txt` directly interface with AI crawlers to establish a verified source of truth.

## Validation Results
✅ Business entity perfectly recognized.
✅ Products and services clearly distinguished.
✅ Machine-readable syntax valid.

## Production Recommendation
**APPROVED**. The site is ready for the era of AI search.
