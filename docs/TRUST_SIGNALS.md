# Trust Signals Document

## Objective
To map and document the factual trust signals implemented across the MushClub domain to build user and search engine confidence.

## Implemented Trust Signals
1. **Physical Location**: The specific physical address (Abishekapatti, Tirunelveli, Tamil Nadu, India) is explicitly stated in both the global footer and the FAQ, grounding the entity in reality.
2. **Transparent Contact Avenues**: Three direct phone numbers and a direct email address are consistently displayed and referenced in inquiries.
3. **Operational Transparency**: Operating hours (8:00 AM – 8:00 PM) and support availability (24/7 Chat Support) are clearly communicated in the FAQ.
4. **Factual Scale**: The business accurately cites its network of "250+ restaurants and caterers" without fabricating arbitrary consumer numbers.
5. **Quality Guarantees**: Explicit, factual claims regarding "chemical-free cultivation", "100% natural hardwood", and "lab-tested purity".

## Excluded Signals (Anti-Patterns)
The following elements were strictly excluded during the content architecture phase to maintain absolute integrity:
- Fabricated customer testimonials or reviews.
- Fake 5-star aggregate ratings.
- Non-existent certifications or awards.
- Fake employee or "Team" profiles.
