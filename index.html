About Us

MushClub was born from a deep passion for mycology and a commitment to bringing the freshest mushroom varieties to your table. What started as a small growing project has blossomed into a dedicated supplier of fresh mushrooms — serving local restaurants, health-conscious families, and mushroom enthusiasts across the region.

We believe that every mushroom tells a story of patience, precision, and care. Our climate-controlled growing rooms, combined with careful attention to mycological purity, ensure that each harvest meets the highest standards of quality and freshness.

Our Mission

To make fresh, organic mushrooms accessible to everyone — from local kitchens to family dinner tables.

Quality Promise

Every batch is lab-tested for purity and potency. We never compromise on quality — from spore to your door.

Customer First

Your satisfaction drives everything we do. Flexible orders, prompt delivery, and a dedicated support team — always.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Everything you need to know about MushClub products, organic cultivation, and delivery.

    What is MushClub?

    MushClub is a premium mushroom cultivation and supply company providing fresh, hygienically grown organic mushrooms, high-yield grain spawn, and hardwood substrates to households, restaurants, and commercial growers.

    Where is MushClub located?

    Our climate-controlled mushroom cultivation facility is located in Abishekapatti, Tirunelveli, Tamil Nadu, India. We deliver farm-fresh daily across the region.

    What mushroom products does MushClub sell?

    We cultivate and supply Fresh Button Mushrooms, Gourmet Oyster Mushrooms, Premium Grain Spawn for growers, and 100% Natural Hardwood Substrate Pellets.

    Are MushClub mushrooms organic and lab-tested?

    Yes. 100% of our mushrooms are grown in clean, chemical-free organic substrates inside sterile, climate-monitored grow rooms. Every harvest is lab-tested for purity.

    Do you provide commercial wholesale delivery?

    Yes! We partner with over 250+ restaurants, caterers, and food retailers, offering reliable cold-chain delivery within 24 hours of harvest.
