About Us

Our Story

MushClub was born from a deep passion for mycology and a commitment to bringing the freshest mushroom varieties to your table. What started as a small growing project has blossomed into a dedicated supplier of fresh mushrooms — serving local restaurants, health-conscious families, and mushroom enthusiasts across the region.

We believe that every mushroom tells a story of patience, precision, and care. Our climate-controlled growing rooms, combined with careful attention to mycological purity, ensure that each harvest meets the highest standards of quality and freshness.

Our Mission

To make fresh, organic mushrooms accessible to everyone — from local kitchens to family dinner tables.

Quality Promise

Every batch is lab-tested for purity and potency. We never compromise on quality — from spore to your door.

Customer First

Your satisfaction drives everything we do. Flexible orders, prompt delivery, and a dedicated support team — always.
