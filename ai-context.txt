## Business Overview
MushClub is a premium mushroom cultivation and supply company located in Abishekapatti, Tirunelveli, Tamil Nadu, India. The company specializes in the organic cultivation and distribution of high-quality fresh mushrooms, mushroom grain spawn, and hardwood substrate pellets. MushClub serves a diverse clientele, including households, over 250+ restaurants and caterers, bulk wholesalers, and commercial mushroom growers. The business operates with a deep commitment to mycological purity, sustainable farming, and delivering the freshest possible product from spore to door.
