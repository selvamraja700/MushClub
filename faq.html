Frequently Asked Questions

What is MushClub?

MushClub is a premium mushroom cultivation and supply company specializing in fresh, organically grown mushrooms for households, restaurants, wholesalers, and commercial growers.

Are your mushrooms organic and lab-tested?

Yes. 100% of our mushrooms are grown in clean, chemical-free organic substrates inside sterile, climate-monitored grow rooms. Every harvest is lab-tested for purity.

Where are you located?

Our climate-controlled grow rooms and main operations are located in Abishekapatti, Tirunelveli, Tamil Nadu, India.

Do you offer wholesale or commercial supply?

Yes, we offer dedicated B2B supply chains for over 250+ restaurants and caterers, providing cold-chain delivery within 24 hours of harvest. We also supply commercial growers with high-yield grain spawn and hardwood substrate pellets.

What are your operating hours?

Our standard operations run from 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM, and we provide 24/7 chat support for our partners and customers.
